# User interview guide (30 minutes)

**Participants:** college students and early earners who use UPI weekly. Aim for 15+. Get consent before recording. For anyone under 18, get guardian consent first.

## Intro (3 min)
Explain the purpose, that there are no wrong answers, and that they can stop anytime. Ask permission to take notes/record.

## Warm-up (4 min)
- Walk me through the last three times you paid with UPI.
- Which apps do you use, and why those?

## Money habits (8 min)
- How do you think about money at the start vs the end of the month?
- Have you ever tried to save a fixed amount? What happened?
- Tell me about the last time you wanted to buy something you couldn't afford yet. What did you do?

## Friction in payments and saving (7 min)
- What annoys you about paying or tracking spending today?
- What would make you trust an app to hold a bit of your money?
- What would make you stop using such a feature?

## Concept reaction (6 min)
Show the prototype or a one-line concept: "Round up each payment into a goal."
- What do you expect would happen? What worries you?
- What amount would feel too small to matter / too big to risk?
- What would make you open it again next week?

## Wrap-up (2 min)
Anything I should have asked? Thank them.

**Rules:** ask open questions, don't pitch, don't lead ("Wouldn't it be great if..."), and record what people did, not only what they said they would do.
